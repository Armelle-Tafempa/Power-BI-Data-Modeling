## 🔎 Data Modeling Concepts Explored

This project covers the following areas of Power BI data modeling:

### 1. Data Model Exploration

Explored the structure and organization of a multi-table data model, identifying the different tables and their roles in supporting business analysis.

### 2. Fact and Dimension Tables

Explored the distinction between fact tables and dimension tables:

- Fact tables: Store business transactions and measurable events.
- Dimension tables: Provide descriptive attributes and context for analysis.

### 3. Dimensional Modeling and Star Schema

Studied dimensional modeling principles and the Star Schema approach, understanding how fact tables connect to dimension tables to create a structured analytical model.

### 4. Relationships and Cardinality

Explored relationships between tables and their role in enabling cross-table analysis, including:

- One-to-many relationships
- Many-to-one relationships
- Relationship cardinality and its impact on data modeling

### 5. Filter Direction

Explored how filter direction influences the propagation of filters across related tables and affects analytical results.

### 6. Data Model Architecture

Worked with a multi-table business data model containing different fact and dimension tables.

**Fact Tables:**
- `fact_sales`
- `fact_inventory`
- `fact_campaign_spend`
- `fact_sales_targets`
- `fact_order_process`
- `fact_promotion_average`

**Dimension Tables:**
- `dim_customer`
- `dim_product`
- `dim_date`
- `dim_geo`
- `dim_campaign`
- `dim_order_flags`

**Additional Table:**
- `security`

### 7. Data Model Visualization

Reviewed the relationships and overall architecture of the data model using Power BI Model View.

The model illustrates how different business entities are connected to support integrated analysis across sales, customers, products, inventory, and marketing campaigns.
